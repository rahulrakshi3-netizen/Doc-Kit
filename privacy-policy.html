Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Our Commitment

Dockit is designed with privacy as its foundation. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app operates entirely offline on your device.

Data Collection

Dockit does not collect any data. There are no analytics SDKs, no crash reporters, no telemetry, and no phone-home mechanisms. All file processing happens locally on your device.

File Storage

All documents you open, convert, or compress remain on your device. Dockit does not upload files to any server. You have full control over your data at all times.

Third-Party Services

Dockit does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app uses no external network connections beyond downloading the app itself.

Open Source

Dockit is fully open source under the GNU General Public License v3. The entire source code is publicly available for audit at github.com/rahulrakshi3-netizen/Doc-Kit.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
